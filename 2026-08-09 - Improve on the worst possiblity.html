Atomic Habit addresses bad days and low motivation moments through three core strategies:

(1) The 2-minute rule (Scaling down habits):
Rather than trying to maintain maximum performance on your worst days, scale your habit down so it takes two minutes or less (e.g. instead of running 5 miles, put on your running shoes). This keeps the neural pathway alive even when energy is at its lowest.

(2) Never miss twice: Missing a habit once is an accident; missing it twice is the start of a new, bad habit. The goal on bad days isn't to hit a personal best, but to maintain continuity.

(3) Focusing on the process/trajectory: James Clear says that small, repeated actions define your identity over time.

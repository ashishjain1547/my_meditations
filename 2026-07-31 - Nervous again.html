A loan is bad for the person who needs it and good for the person who doesn't.

CASE 1:
Netizens have an opinion and view that if you have an X amount of Home loan and you have an X amount of money in your account, you shall not pay the home loan till it has a interest rate like 7-8% set and revised based on RBI's repo rate.

You keep the X amount of money in a fund that has higher returns such as Index funds with (historically) 12-12.5% returns and specifically higher than Home loan interest rate, and then you continue with Home loan.

CLEARLY: This person didn't need home loan but he took it.

CASE 2:
Second case is a person who is okay to pay the EMI and is in need of a house so Home loan would be good for him.

CASE 3:
Third case is a person who DOES NOT want to pay EMIs and who COULD live within or below his means.

I am sort of a Case 3 type of person.
I value freedom more than debts, burdens, EMIs and monthly payouts. 

Home loan is bad for me.

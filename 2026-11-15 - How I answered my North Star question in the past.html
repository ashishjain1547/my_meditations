For a lot of time - I think since my time at Infosys since 2018-19 - I always answered to my last wishes in terms of what others would want "Others" like my mother or my friends at that time it was Geeta Sharma or Shalu Yadav.

And till even to today I was answering "what would I want to do on my last day on Earth?" in terms of expectations that others had from me.

"Others" like my mother and Moni.

And I could find or attribute reasons for this behavior but that doesn't mean my answers were any right.

But let me still present you the justifications: Human being is a social animal. And human being is a problem solver.

When you combine the two, you could get the traits I was exhibiting.

"Solver of other people's problems."

But let me get back to the question : "If I were to die in 24 hours, what would I do?"

And now the thing is it's me who's doing the dying so it should be about me. Not about others.

I think I started asking this special "North Star" question of mine when I was in Infosys. And it was after I had given the presentation on "Steve Jobs" (the biography by Walter Isaacson) in the Infy Book Club.

From 2018 - 2022: I don't have strong recollections of if it was the case everytime but many times

I answered the question like:

# I want to improve my relationship with my mother.
# I want to build my mother a roof over her head (literally).
# I want to improve my connection with Geeta Sharma and Shalu Yadav (my friends at that time).
# I wanted to apologize to anybody or anyone I had been rude to who was undeserving of my bad behaviour.

Around 2020 - 2022: I was at first in extreme suffering from troubles due to a messed up and mixed up personal and professional life with turbulence in most of life's aspects.

And by the time it was 2021-H2, I was full on into nihilistic hedonic way of life, as inspired by the concept of YOLO preached by my then friends like Rohit Sud and Anil Dahiya.

From 2022 onwards, I kept thinking of loans day to day basis but that does not mean I was careful of my spending.

So, lately 2025/26: I was living extravagantly, I was taking care of my family (that's just my mother) and I was slowly-slowly repaying my debts (accumulated 2022-H2 onwards).

And yet: from time to time, I would feel empty, I would feel drowned in a void, I would feel confused.

Also I figured out that my last remaining loan viz. a home loan is not a problem per se, as home loan at 7%-8% interest rate that is also collateralized is considered a good debt.

So upon all this hours of reflection, I realize that its not exactly the loan that's the problem but the monthly EMIs (which I feel like a penalty, a fine).

Now the next steps for me are like as follows:

Grow my emergency fund from "1 month of expenses" to "3 to 6 months of expenses", viz. 3 lacs (@ 1 lac/month) for 3 months to 7.2 lacs @ 1.2 lac/month for 6 months.

And I have about 2.5L in liquid fund: but it's slightly debatable whether that's an emergency fund or savings for medium to long term goals, or an investment.

But I need money now more to maintain a cash flow than to prepay my home loan.

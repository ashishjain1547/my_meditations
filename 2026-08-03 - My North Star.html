So I was totally disengaged and at distance from from my regular and routine life.
Pretty obvious why I am feeling confused!

Asking the "North Star" question again:
What would I do if it were my last day on Earth?

Managing money, managing work and managing family will go on forever till the end of life, but what is it that would matter to me if I were to die?

On my last day on Earth, I would take some time out to savor my little and big wins in life. To thank those I met on the journey to this day. And some time to accept my shortcomings, accept my losses and accept my failures.
